## Translation coverage

The patch covers {{ site.data.patch.translated_entries }} entries across menus, items, furniture, crafting, effects, tutorials, conversations and quests. English provides the meaning and Japanese helps clarify context. Proper names and punctuation-only entries may remain unchanged; new text outside coverage may appear in the selected base language.
